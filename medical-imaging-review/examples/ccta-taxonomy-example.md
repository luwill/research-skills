# Illustrative CCTA Taxonomy

**Last reviewed:** 2026-07-23

This file demonstrates organization only. It is not evidence, does not establish novelty, and must not be cited. Re-run literature searches and verify every named concept before adapting it.

## Possible review question

How do design choices and validation settings affect coronary-artery analysis from CT angiography?

## Possible organization

### Architecture

- Convolutional and encoder-decoder designs
- Transformer and hybrid designs
- Graph and state-space designs where supported by the current corpus

### Inductive prior

- Centerline and topology preservation
- Boundary and shape constraints
- Multi-task vessel, branch, and lesion modeling

### Data regime

- Self-supervised or foundation-model adaptation
- Semi-supervised and weakly supervised learning
- Domain adaptation, federated learning, and multi-center training

### Evidence maturity

- Internal validation
- External-site or temporal validation
- Reader or workflow studies
- Prospective implementation and monitoring

## Adaptation rule

Use only categories supported by the verified corpus. Keep regulatory status, reimbursement, product claims, dataset sizes, and performance numbers out of this example; obtain them from dated official or first-source evidence and record them in `claim_ledger.csv`.
